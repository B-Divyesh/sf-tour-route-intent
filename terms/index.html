Terms for using the route checker

Tour Route Intent records and compares route choices. It does not provide navigation or safety advice.

Using the tool

You may prepare, export, and inspect route files. You must check legal access, conditions, weather, services, and personal ability.

You must also check the behavior of each navigation device or app you use.

Route check limits

The check compares GPX geometry with locked places and both directions of locked segments.

GPS accuracy, simplified files, app changes, and current conditions can affect results.

A passing check does not guarantee safety, access, rideability, or matching turn instructions.
