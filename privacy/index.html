Policy updated 6 September 2026

How your route data is handled

Your route work stays on your device unless you export and share a file.

Data stored on your device

Your current draft and theme are stored in your browser. Paid users can also store named workspaces and a license result.

Imported GPX files and validation stay in the browser. Demo changes use separate session storage and never change your real draft.

Clear this site’s browser storage to remove these records.

Network requests

Static files come from Sociobot hosting. Hosting may keep ordinary security logs, including your IP address, browser, request path, and time.

The app has no analytics, advertising, map tiles, third-party fonts, or runtime CDN requests.

The app contacts api.sociobot.in only after you provide an existing Field kit license.

Exporting and sharing

Route files and notes are not uploaded by the planner. Exported GPX files contain your coordinates and notes.

Anyone who receives an exported file can read that information.

Your choices

You can use GPX import, export, and route checks without an account or purchase.

You can remove a license in the Field kit section. Use browser settings to clear all local route data.

Contact

Send privacy requests to privacy@sociobot.in.
